# Ten free AI video tools I actually tested (2026)

I spent a month running prompts through free tiers to see which tools hand you a usable clip without a card on file, and what they watermark, cap or slow down in return.

## [H3 Video](https://h3video.org/)

Browser-based text-to-video with vertical presets; daily free quota.

## [Seedance 2.5](https://seedance25.kr/)

Korean-friendly prompts, no watermark on the free tier; 40-70s per clip.

## [Kling 4](https://kling4.co.kr/)

Motion brush and lip-sync; up to 10 seconds per generation.

## [Wan 3](https://wan-3.io/)

Pan/tilt/zoom sliders make shots repeatable.

## [Viddi AI](https://viddiai.com/)

Script to captioned vertical video in one workspace.

## [Pavo AI](https://pavoai.app/)

Paste a script, pick avatar or faceless b-roll, get a captioned vertical video.

## [Memories Song](https://memoriessong.com/)

Photos plus a short story become a music-backed memory video.

## [Grok AI Image Generator](https://grokaiimagegenerator.net/)

Fast stills and storyboard frames to animate later.

## [Reverse Video Search](https://reversevideosearch.org/)

Find visually similar videos and their original creators.

## [GTA 6 AI Video](https://gta6aivideo.art/)

Novelty: recreate the GTA 6 trailer look from a prompt.
