Ten free AI video tools I actually tested (2026)

I spent a month running prompts through free tiers to find out which tools hand you a usable clip without a card on file — and what they watermark, cap or slow down in return. Notes below, in the order I'd try them.

  • H3 Video

    Browser-based text-to-video with vertical presets; free tier is capped per day.

  • Seedance 2.5

    Korean-friendly prompts and no watermark on the free tier; 40-70s per clip.

  • Kling 4

    Motion brush and lip-sync; up to 10 seconds per generation.

  • Wan 3

    Pan/tilt/zoom as sliders, so shots are repeatable.

  • Viddi AI

    Script to captioned vertical video in one workspace.

  • Pavo AI

    Paste a script, pick avatar or faceless b-roll, get a captioned vertical video.

  • Memories Song

    Photos plus a short story become a music-backed memory video.

  • Grok AI Image Generator

    Fast stills and storyboard frames to animate later.

  • Reverse Video Search

    Find visually similar videos and their original creators.

  • GTA 6 AI Video

    Novelty: recreate the GTA 6 trailer look from a prompt.
